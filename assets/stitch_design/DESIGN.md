## Brand & Style

This design system re-anchors modern retail commerce around an authoritative, electric cobalt-indigo signature. Moving away from utilitarian discount-orange aesthetics, the visual language communicates premium trust, digital precision, and frictionless transactional velocity.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Premium Utility
- **Visual Personality:** Sharp, vibrant, reliable, and refined. The interface balances high-contrast actionable cues with spacious white-surface framing.
- **Tone:** Confident, sleek, and reassuring. High-end editorial clarity pairs seamlessly with high-conversion e-commerce mechanisms.
- **Design Style:** Modern Corporate meets Neo-Digital Retail—utilizing crisp card structures, tactile interactive pill elements, razor-thin subtle borders, and vivid electric indigo accents.
- **Emotional Response:** High financial trust during checkout, premium brand perception, and effortless clarity during rapid browsing.

## Colors

The color system replaces previous discount warmth with an electric, high-trust chromatic spectrum.

### Role Allocations
- **Primary Electric Indigo (`#4F46E5` / `#4338CA`):** The primary conversion engine. Reserved for critical transactional actions—"Add to Cart", "Instant Checkout", active tab indicators, and verified security focal points. Its deep, luminous saturation commands attention while retaining a sophisticated European tech feel.
- **Secondary Teal (`#0D9488`):** Preserved for positive verification cues—fast delivery indicators, authentic merchant badges, inventory in-stock statuses, and sustainability tags.
- **Tertiary Crimson (`#E11D48`):** High-urgency alert color dedicated to stock depletion warnings, promotional flash tags, and destructive inputs.
- **Neutral Zinc/Slate (`#18181B`):** Deep charcoal anchor for typography, structural high-contrast icons, and primary product headers, avoiding harsh pure black.
- **Supporting Tones:**
  - `brand-indigo-light` (`#EEF2FF`): Soft background tint for active badge fills and highlighted table rows.
  - `brand-indigo-border` (`#C7D2FE`): Soft outline for focused inputs and selected state bounding boxes.
  - `surface-canvas` (`#F8FAFC`): The cool neutral substrate behind floating cards.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E2E8F0`): Clean container delimiters ensuring crisp separation without visual weight.

## Typography

The typography continues using `Plus Jakarta Sans` for its clean, geometric balance and open counters, lending contemporary polish to editorial sections while ensuring maximum legibility on compact mobile viewports.

- **Numerics & Price Formatting:** All monetary and quantitative displays must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee aligned decimal baselines across product grids and itemized checkout receipts.
- **Hierarchy & Titles:** Category hero titles use `headline-lg` (or `headline-lg-mobile` under 600px). Card titles use `headline-sm` clamped to 2 lines to preserve layout rhythm.
- **Price Hierarchy:** Price tags utilize `price-lg` and `price-md` rendered in `#18181B` for baseline pricing, paired with electric indigo highlights (`#4F46E5`) for promotional price tags.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid architecture built for high-throughput mobile retail and adaptive desktop viewing.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 599px):** 4-column fluid grid. Outer margin is `margin` (16px), column gutter is `gutter` (12px). Product catalogs render in a stable 2-column format.
- **Tablet (600px – 1023px):** 8-column fluid grid with 24px margins and 16px gutters. Product listings expand to 3 or 4 columns.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout bounded by a maximum container width of 1280px. Outer canvas margin is `margin-desktop` (32px), with `gutter-desktop` (24px).

### Spacing Principles
Padding and spacing strictly follow 4px base increments. Internal card padding stays compact at `space-md` (12px) on mobile cards to maximize item density, expanding to `space-lg` (16px) across desktop containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses crisp hairline borders and subtle indigo-tinted drop shadows rather than heavy generic gray shading, maintaining high transparency and clarity across devices.

### Hierarchy Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC`, presenting a neutral, low-glare canvas.
- **Surface Level 1 (Card & Module Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background bound by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Flat at rest.
- **Surface Level 2 (Hover States, Floating Action Panels, Mobile Bottom Bar):** Pure `#FFFFFF` fill supported by a subtle indigo-neutral shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers, Modals, Checkout Sheets):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting above a backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.5)`), elevated with `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains soft, rounded geometry that balances human approachability with geometric structure.

- **Base Controls (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to form fields, search containers, segmented filters, and inline metadata badges.
- **Medium Elements (0.75rem / 12px):** Applied to dropdown menus, tooltips, and nested item modules.
- **Large Containers (1rem / 16px):** Applied to product cards, promo panels, and checkout sheet containers.
- **Pill Geometry (9999px):** Applied to core call-to-action buttons, quick-filter chips, and floating navigation bars for immediate tactile affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Cart", "Checkout"):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 48px (mobile) / 52px (desktop). Solid electric indigo (`#4F46E5`), active/hover state deep cobalt (`#4338CA`). Typography: `label-lg` in `#FFFFFF`. Includes subtle glow on hover: `0 4px 14px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Action:** Pill-shaped, transparent background with `1.5px solid #18181B` border and `#18181B` typography.
- **Tertiary / Subtle Action:** Pill-shaped, background `brand-indigo-light` (`#EEF2FF`), text `#4F46E5` (`label-md`).
- **Destructive:** Solid `#E11D48` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Product Cards
- **Shell:** `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `1rem` corner radius. Soft transition to `0 8px 20px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.06)` on hover.
- **Image Module:** 1:1 ratio container with cool white-slate backing (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Badges:** Floated top-left inside media area. Promotional discounts use `#E11D48` text on light crimson ground; verified/new badges use `#4F46E5` on `#EEF2FF`.
- **Pricing:** Dominant presentation using `price-md` in `#18181B` with original strike-through prices in `#64748B`.
- **Quick-Add Trigger:** 36px circular button floating in the bottom-right corner with `#4F46E5` background and a pure white plus icon.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Global Search:** Height 44px, pill-shaped or `rounded-lg` (8px). Background `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E8F0`. Focused state: `2px solid #4F46E5` with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.15)` ring.
- **Form Controls:** 48px height, `#FFFFFF` surface with `#E2E8F0` border. Focus ring invokes `#4F46E5`. Error state uses `1.5px solid #E11D48`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Standard Inactive:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#64748B` (`label-md`).
- **Active Selection:** Background `#4F46E5`, border `1px solid #4F46E5`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Deals / Promo Filter:** Background `#EEF2FF`, border `1px solid #C7D2FE`, text `#4F46E5`.

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 20px x 20px with 4px border radius. Selected state: solid `#4F46E5` fill with white checkmark. Unselected: `1.5px solid #CBD5E1` on white ground.
- **Radio Buttons:** 20px outer circle. Selected state shows a 6px solid `#4F46E5` dot within a `#4F46E5` ring.

### Floating Sticky Checkout & App Navigation
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation:** Fixed 64px bar with `#FFFFFF` background and `1px solid #E2E8F0` top border. Active icons and indicators illuminate in `#4F46E5`, inactive tabs remain `#64748B`.
- **Sticky Checkout Bar:** Floating panel elevated 12px from viewport base with 16px radius, containing item total on left and a full-pill `#4F46E5` checkout action button on right.
